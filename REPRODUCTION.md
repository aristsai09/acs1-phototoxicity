## Environment

The study environment is preserved in `environment/conda_explicit_phototox.txt`, `environment/conda_list_phototox.txt`, `environment/pip_freeze_current.txt`, and `environment/python_version.txt`.

## Historical pipeline caveat

The recovered base pipeline configuration contains five scaffold-CV repeats. The final locked robustness package contains ten repeats. The standalone driver that extended the analysis to ten repeats was not recovered. For this reason, exact final OOF predictions and fold identifiers are the authoritative publication artifact; the archive does not fabricate missing historical code.
